THOUGHT:
The paper is a five-point teaching note. Its arithmetic checks out: residuals are all 3, the mean residual is 3, and MSE/MAE at b = 0, 1.5, and 3 are 9/3, 2.25/1.5, and 0/0. The authors also state plainly that the zero error is in-sample and that the note has no held-out evaluation. The problem is that a constant-residual dataset is zero-error after correction by construction, so the result carries almost no information. The contribution is marginal. Clarity is good, but Section 2 is padded with references that the calculation never uses (seasonal adjustment, classifier comparison, adaptive data analysis, weather-electricity regression). The bibliography also has metadata defects I can see in the text: several entries have identical start and end pages (for example [1] 141–141, [4] 791–791, [10] 15–15, [16] 310–310, [20] 299–299, [24] 370–370, [25] 239–239, [27] 931–931), and [33] has no page range. I worked from the text only, so I could not inspect the figures or the referenced R/TikZ sources. I rate the paper Reject, with Overall 3 for marginal contribution and Confidence 4 because the arithmetic is verifiable but the significance judgement is subjective. The most useful feedback is to extend the note to noisy or held-out data where the correction does not reach zero, and to repair the reference metadata.
