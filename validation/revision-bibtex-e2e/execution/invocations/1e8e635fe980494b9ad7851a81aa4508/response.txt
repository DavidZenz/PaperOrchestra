All three reviewers recommend rejection while agreeing that the arithmetic and squared-error decomposition are correct. The decisive concerns are the limited research contribution and the mismatch between the intended teaching objective and the constant-residual example, which cannot demonstrate different mean and median corrections. The meta-review should preserve the distinction between valid general mathematical results and an illustration that does not exercise them meaningfully. In-sample evaluation is appropriately labeled; held-out evaluation would strengthen the teaching treatment but is not necessary to establish the algebra. Artifact verification remains unresolved rather than evidence of an error.
