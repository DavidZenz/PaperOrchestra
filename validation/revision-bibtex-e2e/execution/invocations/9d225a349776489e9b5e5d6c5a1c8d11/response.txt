The note's arithmetic is correct. Every residual is 3, so b = 3 zeroes the error, and the reported values follow from MSE(b) = (3 - b)^2 and MAE(b) = |3 - b|: 9, 2.25, 0 and 3, 1.5, 0 for b = 0, 1.5, 3. The residual-variance decomposition in Section 2 is also correct. The problem is that these are identities, not experimental results. With a constant residual, the mean residual zeroes the error by construction, so the "three-setting comparison" carries no empirical information. The note says so itself: same-sample agreement, no held-out data, no comparative claim. Its candor is to its credit, but that candor also shows the contribution is negligible. Nothing here is original or significant, and it does not reach the rubric's "marginal contribution" level as a research paper. It reads as a teaching handout.

The Related Work section is out of proportion, with about 64 references for a five-point example. Most cited works (ridge regression, GAMs, gradient boosting, conformal prediction, clinical recalibration, weather MOS) are explicitly not used. That is citation padding and could mislead readers about relevance. These need checking, and they matter in a manuscript whose own theme is verified bibliographies. The text also flags a sign-convention difference between Figure 1 and the equations, which adds confusion without adding content.
