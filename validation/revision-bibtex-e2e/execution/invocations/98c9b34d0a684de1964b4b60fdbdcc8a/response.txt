Schematic of the residual-based intercept correction workflow on five synthetic observations. Baseline predictions 2x and observed targets y determine the residuals r_i, from which the mean correction b = 3 is computed. Adding this scalar offset produces corrected predictions 2x + b, which evaluate to zero residual error on the original data. The dashed path highlights the reuse of the five observations, underscoring that this procedure represents an exact finite arithmetic verification rather than predictive generalization.
