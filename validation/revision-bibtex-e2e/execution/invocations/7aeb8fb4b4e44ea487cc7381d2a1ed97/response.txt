The paper also states its scope honestly: the same five points determine b and assess it, so zero error is an in-sample identity and says nothing about unseen data. The problem is the contribution. The only result is that adding the mean of a constant residual removes that residual on the sample it came from, which follows by construction. There is no method, theory, data, or competing comparison, so the paper cannot separate any options. The roughly 60-entry related-work section is mostly a list of loosely connected literature (GAMs, boosting, ridge, conformal prediction, temperature scaling, survival-model calibration) rather than support for specific claims.
