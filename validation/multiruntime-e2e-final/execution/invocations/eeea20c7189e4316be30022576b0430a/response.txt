THOUGHT:
The arithmetic checks out. Every residual y_i - 2x_i equals 3, so the mean residual is b = 3. With ŷ = 2x + b, the error is e_i(b) = 3 - b at every point, which gives MAE(b) = |3 - b| and MSE(b) = (3 - b)^2. That yields 3, 1.5, 0 for MAE and 9, 2.25, 0 for MSE at b = 0, 1.5, 3, matching Table 1. The corrected predictions (-1, 1, 3, 5, 7) equal the targets. Nothing in the numerical content is wrong.

The problem is scope and evaluation. The note fits a single intercept to five noise-free points, then scores it on those same points, so zero error is guaranteed by construction. The authors acknowledge this, but it means the note supports no claim beyond exact in-sample agreement on a degenerate example. The "correction" is the sample mean of residuals with the slope fixed. That is a special case of ordinary least squares for the intercept, and the note never says so or connects it to any literature. The Related Work section has no citations, and the References section is empty. The b = 1.5 setting is included without motivation, and Figure 2 just replots three points from Table 1. The abstract's "reduced MSE from 9 to 0" is a tautology on the training points.

Presentation is clear and well organized, though repetitive. The provenance block appears twice, and the figure files and checksums are not in the text I was given, so I cannot verify them.

As a conference submission this is not a research contribution. It is a worked teaching example. Overall I rate it 1 and recommend Reject. Confidence is high because the judgment rests on verifiable arithmetic and on the paper's own statements of scope.
